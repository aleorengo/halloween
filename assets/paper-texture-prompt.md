# Aged charcoal paper

Tool: built-in image generation. Final asset: `public/images/aged-charcoal-paper.webp`.

## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless repeating background texture for an elegant gothic Halloween invitation website. Create a square, perfectly flat overhead macro photograph of aged charcoal-black cotton paper, very dark, matte and tactile. Delicate paper fibres and fine tooth, faint dusty handling marks, barely visible rubbed patches and irregular smudges, extremely subtle old stains, soft minute creases and scuffs. Blackened antique printing paper with only hints of neutral warm grey and very muted brown-burgundy. The entire image is a uniform substrate texture with very low contrast: it must sit quietly behind cream lettering and a silver botanical border. No objects, no typography, no borders, no large tears, no holes, no bright stains, no dramatic fold lines, no leather texture, no fabric weave, no stone, no glossy lighting, no vignette or directional hotspot. Seamless tileable edges on all four sides, consistent exposure all over. Real photographic paper surface, sophisticated worn archival material, subtly dirty rather than heavily grungy.
