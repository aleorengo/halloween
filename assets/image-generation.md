# Generated website images

All images below were made with the built-in image-generation tool, copied into this project, and served locally. Colour and material references came from the user's images.

- [Engraved gothic doorway](../public/images/gothic-door-frame.webp) — [prompt](gothic-door-prompt.md), built-in image generation with the user's gothic arch and the website's silver botanical frame as references. The crown and threshold retain their proportions; the side engravings repeat at their native scale.

- [Photographic envelope](../public/images/envelope-photographic.webp) — [prompt](envelope-prompt.md).
- [Empty envelope background for the opening animation](../public/images/envelope-empty-photographic.webp) — [prompt](empty-envelope-prompt.md).
- [Transparent silver botanical frame](../public/images/silver-botanical-frame.webp) — [prompt](frame-prompt.md).
- [Muted tarot tabletop photograph](../public/images/halloween-game-muted.webp) — [prompt](game-image-prompt.md).

The cocktail and costume photographs are the user's supplied images. The frame retains its native proportions, with mirrored vertical vine segments that join at matching endpoints. The final lettering is baked into the photographed paper as black letterpress ink, with hidden live text for accessibility.

- [Photographically printed letter](../public/images/envelope-letterpress.webp) — [prompt](letterpress-prompt.md).

- [Worn gothic headline](../public/images/headline-worn-gothic.webp) — [prompt](headline-prompt.md).

- [Readable worn gothic headline](../public/images/headline-worn-gothic-readable.webp) — [edit prompt](headline-readable-prompt.md), built-in image generation.
- [Aged charcoal paper texture](../public/images/aged-charcoal-paper.webp) — [prompt](paper-texture-prompt.md), built-in image generation.

- [Simplified letterpress invitation](../public/images/envelope-letterpress-simple.webp) — [edit prompt](letterpress-simple-prompt.md), built-in image generation.

- [Photographic ruby dice](../public/images/blood-red-dice.webp) — [prompt](dice-prompt.md), built-in image generation with the supplied dice reference.

- [Sealed flap](../public/images/envelope-sealed-flap.webp) and [sealed tray strip](../public/images/envelope-sealed-tray.webp) — not generated; composed from the photographic envelope by [compose-sealed-envelope.py](compose-sealed-envelope.py) so the opening animation keeps one envelope body throughout.
